Browser extension · Chrome and Firefox

FocalReader

A reading spotlight for people with ADHD and dyslexia. It dims the page around the paragraph you're on, so your eyes stop wandering.

  • Smart Window snaps focus to headings, paragraphs and sections, and steps through them from the keyboard. Works in the PDF viewer too.
  • Explain (AI): focus a dense paragraph and get a plain-language version, on web pages and PDFs. Free daily quota, with a Pro plan for more.
  • Note anchoring with import and export. I rewrote annotation matching to remove an O(n²) slowdown as saved notes grow.
